# Third-party components and publication status

- `vendor/PaddleOCR` is copied from the author's existing local PaddleOCR engine. Its Apache-2.0 license is retained at `vendor/PaddleOCR/LICENSE`. Local modifications are described in `vendor/README.md`.
- PaddlePaddle, Ultralytics, PyTorch, OpenCV, Streamlit and FastAPI are dependencies with their own licenses. No blanket license for this project overrides those terms.
- The detector's training source, weight redistribution rights and applicable Ultralytics terms require verification before weight publication.
- The training dataset source is topkek69/vietnamese-license-plate-ocr on Kaggle; its license has not been verified here. Permission to share fine-tuned weights remains unknown.
- Company evaluation data is not licensed for public redistribution here.

No license has been selected for the author's original code without the author's decision. Adding an original-code license must preserve third-party notices and cannot grant rights to data or weights the author does not own.
